# Solar, batteries, inverters and backup

**Confirm with:** YourHome, which is CC BY 4.0, https://www.yourhome.gov.au/energy — especially the pages on photovoltaic systems and batteries. Save them with library/fetch_au.py or into Your files. Installation is for an accredited installer. Grid connection has network rules.

## What a small system is for

Write the loads in watts and the hours you need them. A 10 watt light for five hours is 50 watt-hours. A phone charger is small. A fridge might be 1 to 2 kilowatt-hours a day depending on the box, the room and how often the door opens. An electric kettle is 2000 watts for a few minutes and will humble a small inverter and a small battery. Survival power is lights, communications, a medical device, and maybe the fridge. It is not the clothes dryer.

Panels make power in daylight, less under smoke, cloud, or a film of bushfire ash. Clean ash off only if you can do it from a safe place without climbing a damaged roof. After a fire, the array may be electrically live in the sun even though the street is dead. Do not cut cables. Do not pull connectors apart unless you have been taught to on that equipment. Tell anyone working on the house that there is solar.

## Batteries

A battery stores the day's surplus for the night. Lead-acid batteries die young if you flatten them every night. Lithium batteries need a battery management system and they do not belong in a sealed hot box in the sun. Either chemistry can start a fire if it is crushed, badly charged, or installed against the manufacturer's clearances. Follow the installation sheet in Your files. Do not invent a charge voltage.

Keep batteries out of living rooms if the manufacturer says so. Many home batteries have required locations, away from bedrooms and exits. That is fire safety, not decoration.

## Inverters and generators

A small inverter turns battery DC into AC for ordinary appliances. Motors and compressors draw a starting surge bigger than their running watts. The inverter has to be sized for the surge, or the fridge will not start and the inverter will fault.

Do not connect an inverter and a generator to the same switchboard without the proper changeover and interlocking, fitted by a licensed electrician. Backfeeding the grid can kill someone repairing the line. See the blackout note for where a petrol generator is allowed to run: outdoors, never in a garage, never as an indoor heater.

A solar system does not make a house safe to occupy in a bushfire. It does not pump unlimited water. It does not replace leaving early.

## Priority when the battery is low

1. Phones and the radio, so you can hear a warning and call 000.
2. Lights, so you are not using candles.
3. A medical device, on its own plan, with a paper copy of what the person needs.
4. The fridge, if the battery can hold it without dropping the items above.
5. Everything else waits.

Write that order down. In a hot night it is easy to spend the battery on a fan and have nothing left for the phone at dawn.
