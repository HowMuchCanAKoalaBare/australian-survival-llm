Australian Survival LLM

A fork of survival-llm. The assistant runs on your computer, with Ollama and a SQLite library, and cites the passage it used. This page does not run the model.

Call 000 for police, fire or ambulance. SES storm and flood: 132 500. Poisons: 13 11 26.

The repo is HowMuchCanAKoalaBare/australian-survival-llm. Read README.md, SOURCES.md and AUSTRALIAN_SOURCES.md. Build the field notes with python3 library/build_library.py.

Ready.gov, CDC, MedlinePlus, US military manuals and the USDA canning guide are not in this library.
